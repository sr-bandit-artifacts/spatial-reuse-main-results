Source components
=================

The ns-3.35 release is bundled in code/vendor/ns-allinone-3.35.tar.bz2 with
SHA256 25e07a95349847b3e453d3af29a94545a4f869b1c6b4d860900cb7718fb1a618.
Its GPL version 2 license is reproduced in code/licenses/ns-3-GPL-2.0.txt.
Individual upstream source files retain their license notices when extracted.

The JsonCpp amalgamated files in code/nsTest retain their public-domain/MIT
license text at the top of json.hh, json-forwards.hh and jsoncpp.cc.

code/patches/ns3-35-block-ack-stability.patch reproduces the two local ns-3
Wi-Fi stability fixes present in the simulator used to generate the results.
No other ns-3 source files differed from the bundled release in the source
audit. These fixes preserve compressed BlockAck support and remove an invalid
iterator after an expired MPDU triggers the discard callback.

code/source_manifest.json records each copied file's original source and
SHA256, its packaged SHA256, and portability or CLI changes. Python core
modules preserve the numerical implementations used for the published full
MLP and QNN. The exposed experiment runner is scripts/run_main.py. Dormant
internal branches in the audited monolithic implementation are not published
experiments, and cannot be selected through the main runner.
